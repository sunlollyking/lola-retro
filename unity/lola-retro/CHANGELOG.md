# Changelog

## [1.12.0] - 2026-10-21

The first Unity version of Lola Retro (version numbers follow the RetroArch version it is made from).

- Every pass, preset and parameter of the RetroArch version, translated from its slang shaders.
- URP (Render Graph), HDRP and the Built-in render pipeline; SDR and HDR displays; XR; WebGL 2.
- The console's picture from the camera's image or from a render texture at the console's resolution.
- The Inspector finds what the render pipeline still needs (URP's renderer feature, HDRP's custom post-process
  order) and fixes it with one button.
- A Demo (the folder Demo in the Asset Store version, a sample in the Package Manager): a test picture through
  every preset, for the Built-in render pipeline and URP.
